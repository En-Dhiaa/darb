# DARB | دَرْب — Corporate Storytelling & Field Documentary Journeys

> "لا نكتفي بأن نحكي قصة شركتك. ننطلق لنعيشها."  
> *"We Don't Just Tell Your Story. We Go Live It."*

DARB is an editorial, field-based documentary media project in Yemen, producing cinematic corporate narratives directly on location.

---

## 🧭 Project Architecture & Routing Conventions

The website is engineered with **Next.js 15 (App Router)**, **TypeScript**, and **Tailwind CSS**, offering ultra-responsive performance, seamless bilingual toggling (Arabic RTL default, English LTR), and strict adherence to editorial integrity.

### Routes:
- `/` — Homepage (Cinematic Hero, Ethos, Three Pillars, Featured Pitch Cards, Founding Team)
- `/how-it-works` — Field Operational Methodology (4-Stage Staging: Reconnaissance, Editorial Brief, Cinema Expedition, Handover)
- `/campaigns` — Editorial Campaign Gallery & Directory (NATCO × JETOUR, From Factory to People, Bespoke Co-Design)
- `/campaigns/natco-jetour` — NATCO Automotive × JETOUR ("جيتور على الطريق" / "JETOUR BEYOND THE ROAD")
- `/campaigns/from-factory-to-people` — Creative Campaign Concept for Manufacturing, FMCG & Distribution ("من المصنع إلى الناس")
- `/partners` — Sponsorship & Partnership Models (No fixed pricing; variable factors; 11 partnership frameworks)
- `/team` — Founding Team Leadership:
  - **ضياء عبدالرحمن الحميري** (*Photographer, Content Creator & Media Project Lead*)
  - **عبدالرحمن علي هباش** (*Professional Driver, Journey Leader & Route Planning*)
- `/contact` — Interactive Multi-Step Partnership Inquiry Form & Direct Executive Channels

## 🛡️ Editorial & Brand Integrity Standards

1. **Clear Proposal Labelling:** All campaign pages are identified as creative pitches (`تصور إبداعي مقترح`), not finalized or approved partnerships.
2. **No Invented Metrics:** No fake follower counts, inflated view counts, or unverified performance guarantees.
3. **No Public Fixed Pricing:** Financial terms are discussed privately and tailored to project variables.
4. **Verified Model Specs:** Vehicle powertrains and capabilities are marked for dealer alignment; no fabricated horsepower, torque, or stunt claims.
5. **Direct Honest Inquiry Transmission:** The 4-step inquiry form includes a full review summary and transmits directly via official WhatsApp or pre-formatted corporate email with zero fake simulated backend APIs.
